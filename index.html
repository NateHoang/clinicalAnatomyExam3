Cranial Nerves
CN Name Nuclei Functional Category What It Does (Function) Pathway / Foramen of Exit
I Olfactory
  • None (no brainstem nucleus) — originates from the cerebrum
  • Special sensory
  • Special sensory — smell
  • Nasal cavity olfactory neurons → olfactory bulb → olfactory tracts → primary olfactory cortex
II Optic
  • None (no brainstem nucleus) — originates from the cerebrum
  • Special sensory
  • Special sensory — vision
  • Retina → optic chiasm (partial decussation) → optic tracts → lateral geniculate nucleus → optic radiations
  • Passes through optic canal
III Oculomotor
  • Oculomotor nucleus (somatic motor)
  • Edinger–Westphal nucleus (visceral/parasympathetic)
  • Somatic motor
  • Visceral motor
  • Somatic motor — 5 extra-ocular muscles:
    • Superior rectus — elevates the eye
    • Medial rectus — adducts the eye
    • Inferior oblique — elevates & abducts the eye
    • Inferior rectus — depresses & adducts the eye
    • Levator palpebrae superioris — raises the upper eyelid
  • Visceral motor:
    • Ciliary muscle — focuses the lens (accommodation for near vision)
    • Sphincter pupillae — constricts the pupil
  • Superior orbital fissure
  • Via ciliary ganglion → short ciliary nerves
IV Trochlear
  • Trochlear nucleus
  • Somatic motor
  • Somatic motor — superior oblique:
    • Abducts
    • Depresses
    • Internally rotates
  • Only nerve arising from dorsal brainstem
  • Superior orbital fissure
V Trigeminal
  • Motor nucleus of trigeminal nerve (branchial motor)
  • Mesencephalic nucleus of trigeminal nerve (somatic sensory)
  • Principal sensory nucleus of trigeminal nerve (somatic sensory)
  • Spinal nucleus of trigeminal nerve (somatic sensory)
  • Branchial motor
  • Somatic sensory
  • Branchial motor and somatic sensory — 3 divisions:
    • V1 Ophthalmic — sensory to upper eyelid, forehead, nose, and scalp
    • V2 Maxillary — sensory to maxilla, palate, nasal cavity, and cheeks
    • V3 Mandibular — sensory and motor to chewing muscles, lower lip, chin, and temporal scalp
  • V1 → Superior orbital fissure
  • V2 → Foramen rotundum
  • V3 → Foramen ovale
VI Abducens
  • Abducens nucleus
  • Somatic motor
  • Somatic motor — lateral rectus
  • Abducts the eye
  • Superior orbital fissure
VII Facial
  • Facial motor nucleus (branchial motor)
  • Superior salivatory nucleus (visceral/parasympathetic)
  • Nucleus of tractus solitarius, superior portion (special sensory — taste)
  • Spinal nucleus of trigeminal nerve (somatic sensory)
  • Branchial motor
  • Visceral motor
  • Somatic sensory
  • Special sensory
  • Branchial motor:
    • Muscles of facial expression
    • Stapedius
    • Stylohyoid
    • Posterior belly of digastric
  • Visceral motor:
    • Lacrimal glands
    • Nasal glands
    • Palatine glands
    • Pharyngeal glands
    • Submandibular glands
    • Sublingual glands
  • Somatic sensory — small area of external ear
  • Special sensory — taste from anterior 2/3 of tongue
  • Geniculate ganglion
  • Exits via stylomastoid foramen
  • Branches:
    • Greater petrosal
    • Stapedius
    • Chorda tympani
VIII Vestibulocochlear
  • Cochlear nuclei (hearing)
  • Vestibular nuclei (balance/equilibrium)
  • Special sensory
  • Special sensory:
    • Hearing — cochlear nerve
    • Balance/equilibrium — vestibular nerve
  • Internal acoustic meatus
IX Glossopharyngeal
  • Nucleus ambiguus (branchial motor)
  • Inferior salivatory nucleus (visceral/parasympathetic)
  • Nucleus of tractus solitarius, superior portion (special sensory — taste)
  • Nucleus of tractus solitarius, inferior portion (visceral sensory)
  • Spinal nucleus of trigeminal nerve (somatic sensory)
  • Branchial motor
  • Visceral motor
  • Visceral sensory
  • Somatic sensory
  • Special sensory
  • Branchial motor — stylopharyngeus:
    • Elevates the pharynx during swallowing
  • Visceral motor — parotid gland
  • Visceral sensory:
    • Carotid sinus baroreceptors
    • Carotid body chemoreceptors
  • Somatic sensory — touch and pain from the pharynx
  • Special sensory — taste from posterior 1/3 of tongue
  • Jugular foramen
X Vagus
  • Nucleus ambiguus (branchial motor)
  • Dorsal nucleus of vagus nerve (visceral/parasympathetic)
  • Nucleus of tractus solitarius, superior portion (special sensory — taste)
  • Nucleus of tractus solitarius, inferior portion (visceral sensory)
  • Spinal nucleus of trigeminal nerve (somatic sensory)
  • Branchial motor
  • Visceral motor (parasympathetic)
  • Visceral sensory
  • Somatic sensory
  • Special sensory
  • Branchial motor — muscles of the pharynx and larynx involved in swallowing
  • Parasympathetic:
    • Heart
    • Lungs
    • Abdominal organs
  • Sensory:
    • Thoracic and abdominal organs
    • Aortic arch baroreceptors
    • Carotid and aortic chemoreceptors
    • Taste buds of the epiglottis
  • Jugular foramen
XI Accessory
  • Spinal nucleus of accessory nerve
  • Branchial motor
  • Branchial motor:
    • Trapezius
    • Sternocleidomastoid
    • Moves the head and neck
  • Carries proprioceptive information
  • Spinal cord → enters skull via foramen magnum → exits via jugular foramen
XII Hypoglossal
  • Hypoglossal nucleus
  • Somatic motor
  • Somatic motor — muscles of the tongue
  • Hypoglossal canal
